This tracker is no longer updated.

The map and counts that were here stopped updating in November 2026. The Australian Government publishes the current picture, and it is the better place to look.

Australian Government: latest bird flu data

It lists confirmed H5 detections by state and is kept current by the Department of Agriculture, Fisheries and Forestry.

To report sick or dead wild birds, call the Emergency Animal Disease Hotline on 1800 675 888.

This was an independent project and was never a government service. The code and the data it collected remain on GitHub.
